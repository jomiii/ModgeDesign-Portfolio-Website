What I Do?
Graphic Design +

Create visually engaging designs for branding, social media, and digital platforms; develop graphic materials that communicate ideas effectively; design layouts, visual assets, and promotional content; explore creative concepts and visual styles; and collaborate on projects to ensure designs are functional, purposeful, and aligned with the intended message and brand identity.

System Analysis (SA) +

Analyze existing systems and business processes; gather and document system requirements; prepare System Analysis Reports (SAR) and Functional Design Documents (FDD); define system modules, functionalities, workflows, user roles, and integrations; and coordinate with developers and stakeholders to ensure system specifications align with project requirements.

Business Analysis (BA) +

Gather and analyze business requirements; understand existing business processes and identify areas for improvement; translate business needs into functional requirements; document workflows and meeting discussions; coordinate with stakeholders, end users, and developers; and ensure system solutions address business objectives and user needs.

QA Analysis (QA) +

Create and execute test cases, test scenarios, and checklists; perform functional and regression testing; identify, document, and report bugs and system inconsistencies; validate actual results against expected results; verify bug fixes and enhancements; and assess system readiness before deployment or release.
